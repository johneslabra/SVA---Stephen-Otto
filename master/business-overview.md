## Target audience
- Who: Vehicle owners looking for reliable, durable, easy-to-install automotive accessories. Core customers include everyday drivers, DIY vehicle owners, Tesla owners, truck/SUV owners, and customers replacing rusted, missing, loose, or incorrect license plate hardware. Secondary audiences include dealerships, body shops, fleet operators, and automotive businesses.
- Pain points:
  - Incorrect screw size or fitment
  - Confusion over vehicle compatibility
  - Rusted or corroded hardware
  - Loose or rattling license plates
  - Missing or stripped screws
  - Cheap hardware that fails over time
  - Theft concerns
  - Difficulty finding the correct hardware for a specific vehicle
  - Poor installation instructions or customer support
- Where they hang out: Amazon Automotive, Google search, YouTube automotive/DIY content, Facebook vehicle-owner groups, Reddit automotive communities, TikTok and Instagram automotive content, and vehicle-specific forums/groups for brands such as Ford, Chevy, Dodge, GMC, Jeep, Cadillac, and Tesla.

2. Increase qualified traffic and sales; support new product launches; grow social media and creator visibility.
3. Position OttoSpeed as a trusted automotive accessory brand rather than a generic marketplace seller.
